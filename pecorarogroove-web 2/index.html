About

Marco Pecoraro is a Venezuelan DJ and producer whose sound is rooted in House, Tech House and mainly Minimal / Deep Tech, delivering groove-driven sets influenced by the underground European club scene.

Inspired by the sound of 90s house and the minimalist groove emerging from the UK, his artistic vision was further shaped after experiencing London's club culture at the iconic Fabric — a pivotal moment that helped define his musical direction.

He later moved to Madrid, where he graduated as a Sound Engineer at the renowned SAE Institute, strengthening both his technical knowledge and creative approach to electronic music.

As a producer, his tracks have started to gain traction within the international underground circuit, receiving DJ support from some of the most respected names in the global house and tech house scene.

Known for his carefully curated selections, rolling grooves and dancefloor-focused energy, Marco Pecoraro continues to develop a sound that connects deeply with club audiences while expanding his presence across the international electronic music scene.
